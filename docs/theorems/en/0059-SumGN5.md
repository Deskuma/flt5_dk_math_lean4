# 0059 — `SumGN5`

## 1. Lean type

```lean
def SumGN5 (u v : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  if v ≤ u then
    (u - v) ^ 4 +
      3 * (u - v) ^ 3 * v +
      4 * (u - v) ^ 2 * v ^ 2 +
      2 * (u - v) * v ^ 3 +
      v ^ 4
  else
    (v - u) ^ 4 +
      3 * (v - u) ^ 3 * u +
      4 * (v - u) ^ 2 * u ^ 2 +
      2 * (v - u) * u ^ 3 +
      u ^ 4
```

`SumGN5` is a definition returning a natural number, not a theorem. For natural numbers `u` and `v`, it gives the residual factor associated with the sum `u+v` in a sum of fifth powers.

## 2. Mathematical statement

The mathematical content of the definition is the symmetric quotient

$$
\frac{u^5+v^5}{u+v}
= u^4-u^3v+u^2v^2-uv^3+v^4.
$$

Because the right-hand side contains negative cross terms, it is awkward to use directly as an expression in `ℕ`. The definition therefore splits into the cases `u≥v` and `v>u`, using the nonnegative difference `u-v` or `v-u` to obtain a positive-coefficient representation.

When `v ≤ u`, put `d=u-v`. Then

$$
\operatorname{SumGN5}(u,v)
= d^4+3d^3v+4d^2v^2+2dv^3+v^4.
$$

In the other branch one sets `d=v-u` and uses the same formula with `u` and `v` exchanged. Thus the piecewise form is not a mathematical asymmetry; it is an implementation device for avoiding signed subtraction in natural numbers.

## 3. Role in the whole proof

In the preceding difference orientation, the difference of fifth powers is factored as

$$
(w-v)\,GN5(w-v,v)=u^5.
$$

In the sum orientation, the equation

$$
u^5+v^5=w^5
$$

must instead be rewritten as

$$
(u+v)\,SumGN5(u,v)=w^5.
$$

`SumGN5` supplies the natural-number residual for this sum side and is the bridge that lets the difference and sum orientations feed the same exact five-adic packet layer.

The generated standalone source describes `SumGN5` as the positive natural quotient `(u^5+v^5)/(u+v)` and explicitly states that the piecewise definition exists only to avoid signed subtraction in `Nat`.

## 4. Direct dependencies

There are no direct dependencies on earlier user-defined declarations. The definition uses only natural numbers, order `≤`, natural subtraction `Nat.sub`, addition, multiplication, powers, and an `if ... then ... else ...` expression.

Mathematically it is the sum-side counterpart of article 0006 `GN5`, and it is introduced to carry the `sumGap` side of article 0052 `SignedBranchAOrientation` into the later five-adic layer.

## 5. Definition flow

1. Test whether `v ≤ u`.
2. If true, use the nonnegative difference `u-v` as the local coordinate and evaluate the positive-coefficient polynomial.
3. Otherwise move to the `u ≤ v` side and use the nonnegative difference `v-u` in the swapped copy of the same residual.
4. The next theorem, `add_mul_sumGN5_eq_add_pow_five`, expands both branches and proves that each satisfies `(u+v)*SumGN5 u v = u^5+v^5`.

## 6. Lean-specific processing

The key Lean-specific point is truncated subtraction on naturals. Unconditionally mixing both `u-v` and `v-u` into one formula would not behave like signed subtraction. The definition first branches on the order and then uses only the guaranteed nonnegative difference.

This keeps both the domain and codomain in `ℕ`, which fits the later use of `padicValNat`, divisibility over naturals, and positivity lemmas.

## 7. Redundancy and duplication

The two branches are nearly identical after swapping `u` and `v`, so there is substantial syntactic duplication. However, this duplication has an auditability advantage: the safety condition for natural subtraction is visible immediately beside the expression that relies on it.

Mathematically one could instead define the single alternating-sign polynomial over `ℤ`, prove nonnegativity, and convert back to `ℕ`. That removes the branch duplication but introduces casts and nonnegativity obligations.

## 8. Optimization candidates

There are three reasonable options.

- Keep the current form, prioritizing explicit natural-number safety and easy downstream positivity.
- Introduce a shared helper polynomial in `d,a`, namely `d^4+3*d^3*a+4*d^2*a^2+2*d*a^3+a^4`, to reduce branch duplication.
- Make the symmetric polynomial over `ℤ` canonical and convert to naturals afterward. This is mathematically compact but increases boundary conversions in the five-adic layer.

For the theorem museum, the current piecewise definition has a clear architectural reason, so shortening the code alone is not a strong reason to change it.
